Our Story

It Began With One Home-Cooked Tiffin

In 2018, Chef Meera started cooking extra portions of her family recipes for a few neighbours who missed home food while working long hours. Word spread fast, and within a year, HomeTaste Kitchen was serving over 200 tiffins a day.

Today we run a licensed cloud kitchen and a full catering wing — but every dish still follows the same home recipes, cooked in small batches with the same care.

Meet Our Home Chef

Chef Meera Krishnan

With 18 years of home cooking and a diploma in culinary nutrition, Chef Meera leads a kitchen team of 12, personally tasting every batch before it leaves for delivery.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you cater for dietary restrictions?
Yes — we accommodate vegetarian, vegan, Jain, and low-spice requests. Just mention it in special instructions when booking.
What areas do you deliver to?
We currently deliver across the city and nearby suburbs within a 15 km radius of our kitchen.
How far in advance should I book catering?
For events over 50 guests, we recommend booking at least 5 days in advance to guarantee your preferred package.
Can I customise a catering package?
Absolutely — use our Interactive Menu Builder on the Catering page to add or remove courses from any package.
